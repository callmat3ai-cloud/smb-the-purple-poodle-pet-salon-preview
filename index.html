Our Story

A Different Kind of Pet Salon

Founded by professional pet stylist Madison Ross, The Purple Poodle was established with a singular, clear vision: to redefine the pet grooming experience. We noticed that traditional salons were loud, chaotic environments full of cages, causing elevated stress levels for dogs and their owners.

"We treat every dog that walks through our doors in Remington like a member of our own family. Safe, slow, and stylish is our promise."

We created a modern, boutique styling studio focused on slow-paced, cage-free wellness. By combining state-of-the-art spa equipment, custom curated organic bathing products, and a calm acoustic atmosphere, we've designed a sanctuary that pets actually look forward to visiting.
